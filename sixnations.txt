THE SIX NATIONS
================

In Six Nations, six factions are placed around a symmetrical map composed of 37 smaller hexes arranged in a large hexagon shape (1 center hex + 3 concentric rings). Each of the six nations starts in control of four corner hexes: one corner hex and the three adjacent hexes.

The six nations are:
  - Yilerond  (Yellow)
  - Galland   (Green)
  - Beldrin   (Sky Blue)
  - Crestmoor (Cobalt)
  - Malkor    (Magenta)
  - Ravengard (Crimson)

All six nations begin the game in mutual neutrality. There are no predefined diplomatic rings or starting alliances. Alliances and wars are forged dynamically during play.


SUMMARY & OBJECTIVE
===================

Six Nations is a strategic game of shifting alliances and hidden agendas for 2 players (human vs bot or human vs human).

Players do not command a single "home" nation. Instead, before the game begins, each player secretly designates their victory predictions from among the six nations:
  - 3 PREVAIL nations: nations predicted to survive (sovereign alive at game end).
  - 3 DEFEAT nations: nations predicted to fall (sovereign destroyed at game end).

These predictions are ranked in priority order (Slot 1 = 3 pts, Slot 2 = 2 pts, Slot 3 = 1 pt). Maximum possible score is 12 points (up to 6 pts for Prevail + up to 6 pts for Defeat).

The game ends immediately when any 3 sovereigns have been destroyed. The player with the highest prediction score wins the game.


GAME SETUP
==========

1. The Board:
   - 37 flat-topped hexes (center hex + 3 surrounding rings).
   - Each of the 6 nations starts in control of 4 adjacent cornermost hexes.

2. Starting Units:
   - Each nation starts with:
     - 1 Sovereign in their cornermost hex.
     - 1 Champion in their cornermost hex (sharing the hex with the Sovereign).
     - 1 Army in each of their 3 adjacent starting hexes (3 armies total).
   - Maximum unit limits per nation:
     - At most 1 Sovereign.
     - At most 1 Champion.
     - At most 1 Knight.
     - Armies capped by controlled territory (base 3, +1 for every 3 hexes controlled beyond 4).

3. Initial Diplomacy:
   - All nations start in a Neutral diplomatic stance toward one another.

4. Secret Predictions:
   - Each player secretly assigns 3 nations to PREVAIL and 3 nations to DEFEAT, ranked across slots 1, 2, and 3.


TURN FLOW & ACTION ECONOMY
==========================

Players alternate turns. On their turn, a player may perform exactly ONE action using pieces or diplomacy of ANY nation, provided the nation is not restricted by cooldowns:

A game turn action is any one of the following:
  1. Move an Army, Knight, Champion, or Sovereign to an adjacent valid hex.
  2. Attack an adjacent enemy unit with an eligible Army, Knight, or Champion.
  3. Recruit a new Army for an eligible nation.
  4. Promote an existing Army to a Champion (if the nation has lost its champion).
  5. Promote an existing Army to a Knight (if the nation does not already have a knight).
  6. Conduct Diplomacy to change or renew stances between two nations.
  7. PASS the turn to the opponent.

COOLDOWNS ON PLAYER MOVES:
To prevent players from repeatedly manipulating the same nation:
  - Player Move Cooldown: A player cannot move a nation they moved on either of their last 2 turns.
  - Global Move Cooldown: A player cannot move the nation that their opponent moved on the immediately preceding turn.
  - A pass move does not generate a cooldown since no nation took a move.


TERM DEFINITIONS & UNIT CAPABILITIES
====================================

UNITS:
  - Army: Standard combat unit. Only one army or knight unit can occupy a hex at one time.
  - Knight: Heavy cavalry unit promoted from an army. Knights cannot be destroyed by unsupported enemy armies and can eliminate unsupported sovereigns. A nation may have at most 1 knight.
  - Champion: An elite offensive hero unit. Fast, versatile, and ignores neutral terrain boundaries. A nation may have at most 1 champion.
  - Sovereign: The monarch of the nation. Sovereigns cannot attack, but they provide support to friendly units in their hex. Sovereigns are the primary targets of the game; when a sovereign falls, their nation collapses into a Ghost Nation.

STACKING IN HEXES:
  - Armies and Knights can NEVER share a hex with any other army or knight.
  - Champions and Sovereigns may share hexes with armies, knights, and other champions/sovereigns, provided all units in the hex belong to the same nation or are allied.
  - Maximum stacking limit per hex is 5 units: 1 army/knight, up to 2 allied champions, and up to 2 allied sovereigns.
  - Units from enemy nations can never peacefully coexist in the same hex.

SUPPORT:
  - A unit is SUPPORTED if it shares a hex with another allied unit of any type (same nation or declared ally).
  - Because armies and knights cannot share hexes with other armies/knights, an army or knight is only supported if accompanied by a champion or sovereign.
  - A unit alone in a hex is UNSUPPORTED.


MOVEMENT & TERRAIN ENTRY RESTRICTIONS
=====================================

Movement is to an adjacent hex that contains no enemy units. In addition, terrain control imposes strict entry restrictions based on unit type:

1. Army & Knight Movement:
   - Destination hex must not already contain an army or knight.
   - May freely enter: Own nation's territory, Allied territory, Enemy territory, or Unclaimed/neutral hexes.
   - NEUTRAL RESTRICTION: Armies and Knights CANNOT enter hexes controlled by a nation with which they are Neutral. Crossing into foreign borders requires an alliance or a declaration of war.

2. Champion Movement:
   - Champions have complete operational freedom. They may enter any adjacent hex (own, ally, neutral, enemy, or unclaimed), provided no enemy unit occupies it.
   - Champions may freely traverse neutral-controlled territory without declaring war.

3. Sovereign Movement:
   - Sovereigns are subject to strict royal lockdown:
     - OWN TERRITORY ONLY: A sovereign can ONLY move into adjacent hexes currently controlled by their own nation. A sovereign can never leave their home borders or enter neutral, allied, or enemy hexes.
     - MANDATORY SUPPORT: A sovereign may ONLY move into a hex that is already occupied by a friendly unit (must be supported upon arrival). A player can never walk a sovereign into an empty or isolated hex.

4. NEUTRAL SOVEREIGN PROTECTION (Universal Rule):
   - No unit of any type (Army, Knight, Champion, or Sovereign) may move onto a hex that is currently occupied by the sovereign of a nation with which the moving unit's nation is Neutral.
   - This restriction applies even to Champions, who are otherwise free to enter neutral territory.
   - To enter a hex containing a neutral sovereign, the moving nation must first declare War or Alliance with that sovereign's nation:
     - Declaring WAR makes the hex a valid attack target (not a move target).
     - Declaring ALLIANCE makes the hex a valid move/stack target.
   - Moving onto a hex containing an allied sovereign is always legal (units of allied nations may share hexes).
   - Moving onto a hex containing an enemy sovereign is handled by the attack system, not the move system.

DIPLOMACY SYSTEM
================

Diplomacy allows nations to forge alliances, declare wars, or sue for peace. Stances between any pair of nations are symmetric (Nation A's stance toward Nation B is always identical to Nation B's stance toward Nation A).

DIPLOMATIC STANCES:
  - Neutral: Default starting state. Nations coexist peacefully but armies/knights cannot enter neutral borders. Units cannot attack neutral pieces.
  - Ally: Friendly relationship. Armies and knights may enter allied territory. Units can share hexes and provide support to one another. Allied units cannot attack each other.
  - Enemy: Active state of war. Units can attack enemy pieces, seize enemy hexes, and cannot share hexes.

DIPLOMATIC ACTIONS:
Conducting diplomacy is a full turn action:
  - Declare Alliance: Sets stance to Ally. Initiates a secret cooldown of 10 to 12 turns locking the relationship.
  - Declare War: Sets stance to Enemy. Initiates a secret cooldown of 10 to 12 turns locking the relationship.
  - Restore Neutrality: Ends an alliance or war, returning the relationship to Neutral. Takes effect immediately with NO cooldown lock.
  - Renew Stance: Re-locks an active war or alliance for another 10 to 12 turns.

WAR DECLARATION RESOLUTION:
When war is declared between two nations whose units currently share hexes (e.g., coexisting former allies or overlapping champions):
  1. Immediate Combat Resolution:
     - Armies vs Armies, Knights vs Knights, and Armies vs Knights combat resolves immediately on shared hexes according to standard combat rules.
     - Champions engage eligible enemy units.
     - Unsupported sovereigns in shared hexes are destroyed.
  2. Territory Seizure:
     - If a hex controlled by Nation A is occupied solely by units of newly hostile Nation B, Nation B immediately seizes control of the hex (and vice-versa).


COMBAT & ATTACKS
================

Only Armies, Knights, and Champions can initiate attacks.
Units can only attack ENEMY units (never neutral or allied units).
An attack is directed at an adjacent enemy hex.

AUTO-TARGETING PRIORITY:
When attacking a hex containing multiple enemy units, attacks automatically resolve against the highest-priority target:
  Army  >  Knight  >  Champion  >  Sovereign
A player cannot bypass a protecting army or knight to strike a champion or sovereign.

ADVANCE AFTER COMBAT:
A surviving attacking unit that destroys its target must advance into the defended hex, unless prevented by stacking rules (e.g., another army already present).

NO SOVEREIGN TRAPPING:
The obsolete rule requiring sovereigns to be "trapped on opposite sides" has been completely removed. Any unsupported enemy sovereign can be attacked and destroyed directly.

COMBAT RESOLUTION MATRIX:

1. Army Attacks:
   - vs Enemy Army:
     - Unsupported Army vs Unsupported Army: Both armies destroyed.
     - Unsupported Army vs Supported Army: Illegal move (cannot attack).
     - Supported Army vs Unsupported Army: Defender destroyed; attacker advances.
     - Supported Army vs Supported Army: Both armies destroyed.
   - vs Enemy Knight:
     - Unsupported Army vs Knight: Illegal move (cannot attack).
     - Supported Army vs Unsupported Knight: Knight destroyed; army advances.
     - Supported Army vs Supported Knight: Both units destroyed.
   - vs Enemy Champion:
     - Illegal move (armies cannot attack champions).
   - vs Enemy Sovereign:
     - Army vs Unsupported Sovereign: Sovereign destroyed; army advances.
     - Army vs Supported Sovereign: Illegal move (cannot attack).

2. Knight Attacks:
   - vs Enemy Army:
     - Unsupported Knight vs Unsupported Army: Both units destroyed.
     - Unsupported Knight vs Supported Army: Illegal move (cannot attack).
     - Supported Knight vs Unsupported Army: Army destroyed; knight advances.
     - Supported Knight vs Supported Army: Both units destroyed.
   - vs Enemy Knight:
     - Unsupported Knight vs Unsupported Knight: Both knights destroyed.
     - Unsupported Knight vs Supported Knight: Illegal move (cannot attack).
     - Supported Knight vs Unsupported Knight: Enemy knight destroyed; attacker advances.
     - Supported Knight vs Supported Knight: Both knights destroyed.
   - vs Enemy Champion:
     - Illegal move (knights cannot attack champions).
   - vs Enemy Sovereign:
     - Knight vs Unsupported Sovereign: Sovereign destroyed; knight advances.
     - Knight vs Supported Sovereign: Illegal move (cannot attack).

3. Champion Attacks:
   - vs Enemy Army:
     - Unsupported Champion vs Unsupported Army: Army destroyed; champion advances.
     - Unsupported Champion vs Supported Army: Illegal move (cannot attack).
     - Supported Champion vs Supported Army: Defender army destroyed; champion advances if hex is cleared.
   - vs Enemy Knight (only if no enemy army present):
     - Unsupported Champion vs Unsupported Knight: Knight destroyed; champion advances.
     - Unsupported Champion vs Supported Knight: Illegal move (cannot attack).
     - Supported Champion vs Unsupported Knight: Knight destroyed; champion advances.
     - Supported Champion vs Supported Knight: Knight destroyed; champion does not advance if other units block.
   - vs Enemy Champion (only if no enemy army/knight present):
     - Unsupported Champion vs Unsupported Champion: Both champions destroyed.
     - Unsupported Champion vs Supported Champion: Illegal move (cannot attack).
     - Supported Champion vs Unsupported Champion: Defender destroyed; attacker advances.
     - Supported Champion vs Supported Champion: Both champions destroyed.
   - vs Enemy Sovereign (only if no enemy army, knight, or champion present):
     - Unsupported Champion vs Unsupported Sovereign: Sovereign destroyed.
     - Supported Champion vs Unsupported Sovereign: Sovereign destroyed.
     - Supported Champion vs Sovereign supported by another Sovereign: Defending sovereign destroyed.
     - Champion vs Sovereign shielded by a Champion: Illegal move (cannot attack).


RECRUITMENT & PROMOTIONS
========================

RECRUITMENT (MUSTER ARMY):
A player may recruit a new Army for a non-ghost nation as their turn action, provided:
  1. Capacity Limit: The nation has not reached its army capacity (3 base + 1 per 3 controlled hexes beyond 4).
  2. Recruitment Cooldown: The nation's individual 10-turn recruitment cooldown has elapsed. Each nation tracks its own recruit cooldown independently (1 recruit per 10 player turns).
  3. Valid Hex: The placement hex is currently controlled by that nation, contains no existing army or knight, and has NO enemy units on or adjacent to it.
The recruitment button on the sidebar outside the nation's corner hex only appears when BOTH the 10-turn cooldown has elapsed and an eligible hex exists.

CHAMPION PROMOTION:
If a non-ghost nation has lost its champion, an army located in territory controlled by that nation may be promoted to Champion as a turn action (max 1 champion per nation).

KNIGHT PROMOTION:
An army located in territory controlled by that nation may be promoted to Knight as a turn action, provided the nation does not already have a knight (max 1 knight per nation).


TERRITORY CONTROL & GHOST NATIONS
=================================

CLAIMING & SEIZING TERRITORY:
  - Neutral / Unclaimed Hexes: An Army, Knight, or Champion that moves into an unclaimed or neutral hex immediately claims it for their nation.
  - Enemy Hexes: An Army, Knight, or Champion that moves into an enemy-controlled hex immediately seizes control of it.
  - Allied Hexes: Moving into allied territory does not alter ownership ("first come keeps it").
  - Sovereigns: Moving a sovereign never alters hex ownership.

GHOST NATIONS & TERRITORY COLLAPSE:
When a sovereign is destroyed, that nation immediately becomes a Ghost Nation:
  - All territory controlled by the fallen nation is immediately released at the moment of collapse:
    - Any hex occupied solely by units of another nation transfers ownership to that nation.
    - Any hex that is empty, or occupied by units of two or more nations, reverts to neutral/unclaimed.
  - Ghost nations can never recruit new armies, promote champions, or promote knights.
  - Ghost nation pieces remain on the board; players may still maneuver or sacrifice them on future turns subject to normal move cooldowns.
  - Ghost nation diplomacy: A ghost nation cannot initiate diplomacy, but may be the target of diplomacy from other nations.

VICTORY & SCORING: PREVAIL / DEFEAT
===================================

GAME END TRIGGER:
The game ends immediately as soon as THREE SOVEREIGNS HAVE BEEN DESTROYED (leaving 3 surviving sovereigns and 3 ghost nations).

FINAL SCORING:
At game end, both players reveal their secret PREVAIL and DEFEAT predictions made before the match:
  - PREVAIL Predictions (Nations predicted to survive):
      Slot 1 (Priority #1): +3 points if sovereign is alive.
      Slot 2 (Priority #2): +2 points if sovereign is alive.
      Slot 3 (Priority #3): +1 point  if sovereign is alive.
  - DEFEAT Predictions (Nations predicted to be eliminated):
      Slot 1 (Priority #1): +3 points if sovereign is dead (Ghost Nation).
      Slot 2 (Priority #2): +2 points if sovereign is dead (Ghost Nation).
      Slot 3 (Priority #3): +1 point  if sovereign is dead (Ghost Nation).

Maximum possible score is 12 points (6 points from Prevail + 6 points from Defeat).

WINNER DETERMINATION:
The player with the higher total score wins. If both players achieve identical point totals, the game ends in a tie.
